# Sign-off: legal pages, About and FAQs

Drafts written 26 Nov 2026 for Tier One, a company registered in Ireland. Every [square bracket] on the
site is a question below. Answers go into `lib/site.ts` (`site.company`) or the page named. The whole set
needs independent legal review before it is relied on; none of it is legal advice.

Pages: `/privacy`, `/cookies`, `/terms`, `/booking-conditions` (kept out of search until signed off),
`/about`, `/faqs`.

## Company (fill once, used everywhere)

- [ ] Registered company name (e.g. "Tier One Sports Events Limited")
- [ ] CRO number
- [ ] Registered office address
- [ ] VAT number, if registered
- [ ] Travel licence (Commission for Aviation Regulation) and bond or other insolvency protection

Once name, number and office are in, the footer shows them, as Irish company law requires.
